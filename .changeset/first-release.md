First release: country and subdivision maps from a JSON spec a model can write, checked by `parse` and `draw` and drawn as SVG. It ships 242 pre-projected sheets, points at 7,267 Natural Earth places, the `<map-chart>` element, the `minimal-map-charts render` CLI and llms.txt.
